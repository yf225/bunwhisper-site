BunWhisper

A personal Mac app that records meetings and turns them into transcripts.

BunWhisper is used by its developer and a few people they know personally. It is not sold or offered to the public. It records a call (your microphone and the call's sound), transcribes it on the user's own computers, and saves the transcript on the Mac (and, if the user turns it on, in their own Notion workspace).

Google Calendar (optional)

Each Mac can sign in to one Google account. BunWhisper then reads (never changes) that account's calendars to find the meeting a recording belongs to, so the recording can be named after the meeting and the attendees' names can be offered when the user names who spoke. Details are in the privacy policy.
