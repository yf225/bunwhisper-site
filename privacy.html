BunWhisper Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

BunWhisper is a personal Mac app for recording meetings and transcribing them. It is used by its developer and a few people they know personally. This policy explains what it does with data, in particular data from Google.

Google user data BunWhisper accesses

Signing in to Google is optional. When a user signs in on a Mac, BunWhisper asks for read-only access:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly: the list of the account's calendars;
  • calendar.events.readonly: the events in those calendars (title, times, location, description, organizer, attendees' names and email addresses, response status, and video-call links);
  • openid and email: the account's email address, to show which account is signed in.

BunWhisper never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.

How the data is used

  • To find the meeting a recording belongs to (the event that overlaps the recording), so that the recording and its transcript can be named after the meeting.
  • To start or offer a recording when a call that is in the calendar begins (for example a Zoom or Google Meet link in the event).
  • To offer the attendees' names when the user names the speakers of a transcript.

The data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to build profiles. No person reads it other than the user.

Where the data goes and how it is stored

  • Calendar events are read directly from Google by the app on the user's Mac and kept in the app's memory.
  • The sign-in (OAuth tokens) is stored on that Mac, in a file only the user's macOS account can read.
  • The meeting's title and attendees may be written into the recording's transcript files on the user's Mac.
  • To suggest who spoke, the attendees' names and email addresses are sent together with the transcript to the user's own transcription computers on the user's private network. They are not sent to any third party.
  • If the user turns on the Notion import, transcripts (which may contain the meeting title and speaker names) are saved in the user's own Notion workspace.

BunWhisper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • "Sign Out" in BunWhisper's settings deletes the stored sign-in from the Mac and asks Google to revoke it.
  • Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Recordings and transcripts are ordinary files on the user's Mac (and pages in their Notion workspace) that the user can delete.
